# Funding scan for ALEA

Scanned 2026-11-25 from web search snippets and two fetched pages. Nothing here was read on a funder's own application page except where noted, so every amount and date needs re-checking before use. Pages were not archived; add copies to `sources/` before any figure is cited in a proposal.

## What ALEA offers a funder

MIT licence, open platform, 55 AU member states and five power pools, long-run planning plus operations, parameter graph with 1,881 nodes (0 uncoupled), PyPSA-based planning core. Kenya M1 solves optimally on windows up to 91 days; the full-year solve and M4 to M8 are open (see `docs/IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md`). A funder will ask for the delivered part, so lead with Kenya and the PRG.

## Not funders, but routes in

- Open Energy Transition (PyPSA developers): no call found; a partner, since ALEA builds on PyPSA. Ask about joint proposals.
- Climate Compatible Growth / EMP-A and OpenMod4Africa (Horizon Europe, 2023 to 2026): peer projects building African open models. ALEA should be positioned against them, and a collaboration is likelier than a grant. Their toolbox is at africanenergymodels.net.

## Read of the scan

- No open call matches ALEA today. The search found closed rounds and partner routes, not live deadlines.
- Most plausible near-term: EnAccess (once the conflict is settled by asking), then a Climate Change AI round, then a European consortium for LEAP-SE 2027.
- Unchecked: Wellcome-style philanthropies do not apply; Gates, GEAPP, SEforALL, FCDO, GIZ, EU Global Gateway, IRENA, Schmidt Sciences and Google.org were searched only in aggregate, and none surfaced an open call.
