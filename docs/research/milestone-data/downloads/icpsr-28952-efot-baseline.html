    The Ghana II ECG Financial and Operational Turnaround (EFOT) evaluation collected weekly values of four quantitative measures of electricity quality and reliability (SAIDI, SAIFI, voltage, and undervoltage). Baseline data was collected from June 2018 to August 2021 across 426 sites in 21 ECG districts that were equipped with GridWatch sensors.
