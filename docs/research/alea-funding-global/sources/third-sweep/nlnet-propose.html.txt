Next deadline January 3, 2027 Apply for a grant Next deadline: January 3 rd 2027 12:00 CET (noon) Through this form you can request financial support for your project. NLnet runs several funds with different themes and eligibility requirements, so learn more about our funding and see if any fund matches. Some general requirements: Our grants are primarily for technical development . Software, hardware and content must be released under open licences and apply (preferably open) standards . Proposals and projects should be made by humans, not AI . See our Generative AI policy . Our EU-supported funds require a strong European dimension ; usually this means applicants should (mostly) be from Horizon Europe-eligible countries. Please be short and to the point in your answers; submitting a proposal can be quick! Note that you will need to validate your email address before you can submit. Please answer in plain text and in English, in your own words . We don't care about spelling or grammar, only about your ideas. If you require AI-assistance for accessibility purposes , keep your prompts/interactions and submit your logs below.
