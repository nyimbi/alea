Introduction

Mozilla Foundation will award 10 Fellowships for the 2027-2028 period. We are looking for extraordinary individuals working at the edges of human imagination, on technology that's too early, too unexpected or too ambitious for anyone else to fund.

Our Fellows are builders first, of tools and of the fields around them. But they are also defiant about who technology should serve, and rigorous about making it a reality.

If that sounds like you, come build with us. Bring the wildest, most radical version of your idea: the one that questions who gets to decide and puts control back in the hands of the people who use it. Optimizing the status quo is somebody else's job. Ours is to build the alternatives.

Before completing the application, we recommend reviewing the full RFP here.

Letters of Intent are due on December 17, 2026 @ 11:59PM PST / UTC-8.

Please note: If you provide your email address, Mozilla Foundation may send you reminders related to your application, including reminders to complete and submit it before the deadline. For more information about how we handle your personal information, please review our Privacy Notice.

AI and Translation Disclosure*

We recognize that applicants may use tools such as AI to help brainstorm ideas, clarify language, or assist with translation. These tools can support the application process, especially for non-native English speakers or those seeking to improve clarity.

However, all submitted content must ultimately reflect your own original work, ideas, and voice. The use of AI or translation tools should be limited to supportive functions, not to generate full responses or replace your personal input. Applications that appear to be heavily or entirely AI-generated, or that do not demonstrate authentic engagement with the application questions, may be subject to further review.
