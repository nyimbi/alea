 How to apply | Catalyst Fund Skip to content STATUS: Applications open Apply now → How to apply Applications for exploratory grants are open. We ask for one short proposal, due December 31, 2026. No institutional affiliation is required, and applications are welcome from anywhere in the world. Apply now → Who can apply Individuals, informal teams, institutions, and non-profits, anywhere in the world, unless US sanctions law prevents us from paying them. See what we cannot fund . Teams formed at the Edinburgh workshop, and teams with no connection to it. Both are equally welcome. Applicants who cannot receive funds through an institution: the fund is fiscally hosted at Hack Club Bank and can pay individuals and unincorporated groups. Not LLCs, companies, or other for-profit organisations. See the FAQ . What we will ask for Five pages, maximum. Plain language. What you will do, and what exists at the end of it. Who is involved and what they bring. A simple budget and a schedule of three to six months. Indirect costs (overheads) can be up to 10% of the budget. How it connects to the priorities the community has already identified, and why it matters if it does not. How proposals are reviewed Submitted. You can send the form at any time before the deadline on December 31, 2026. Read by the panel. Five to seven community reviewers, none of whom are applicants in this round. Decision. The panel reviews proposals from January to March 2027, and applicants are notified of the decision by mid-March 2027: yes, no, or a request to revise. We cannot provide individual feedback on proposals. Funds released. Paid through Hack Club Bank once the paperwork is complete. Programme chair Karthik Ram UC Berkeley [email protected] For more on decisions and feedback, read the FAQ . What reviewers look for Readiness. Can this start now and finish inside six months? A concrete output. Something the community can read, run, teach, or reuse, released under an open license: OSI-approved for software, CC BY or CC0 for everything else. Relevance. It advances the practice of producing trustworthy research software with AI. Who benefits. Beyond the applicants themselves. Proportionate budget. The cost matches the work.
