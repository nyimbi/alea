How to apply

# Applications for exploratory grants are open.

We ask for one short proposal, due December 31, 2026. No institutional affiliation is required, and applications are welcome from anywhere in the world.

[Apply now →][1]
## Who can apply

* Individuals, informal teams, institutions, and non-profits, anywhere in the world, unless US sanctions law prevents us from paying them. See [what we cannot fund](/what-we-fund/#what-we-cannot-fund).
* Teams formed at the Edinburgh workshop, and teams with no connection to it. Both are equally welcome.
* Applicants who cannot receive funds through an institution: the fund is fiscally hosted at Hack Club Bank and can pay individuals and unincorporated groups.
* Not LLCs, companies, or other for-profit organisations. See the [FAQ](/faq/#for-profit).

## What we will ask for

* Five pages, maximum. Plain language.
* What you will do, and what exists at the end of it.
* Who is involved and what they bring.
* A simple budget and a schedule of three to six months. Indirect costs (overheads) can be up to 10% of the budget.
* How it connects to the priorities the community has already identified, and why it matters if it does not.

## How proposals are reviewed

1.  **Submitted.** You can send the form at any time before the deadline on December 31, 2026.
2.  **Read by the panel.** Five to seven community reviewers, none of whom are applicants in this round.
3.  **Decision.** The panel reviews proposals from January to March 2027, and applicants are notified of the decision by mid-March 2027: yes, no, or a request to revise. We cannot provide individual feedback on proposals.
4.  **Funds released.** Paid through Hack Club Bank once the paperwork is complete.

### What reviewers look for

* **Readiness.** Can this start now and finish inside six months?
* **A concrete output.** Something the community can read, run, teach, or reuse, released under an open license: [OSI-approved][2] for software, [CC BY][3] or [CC0][4] for everything else.
* **Relevance.** It advances the practice of producing trustworthy research software with AI.
* **Who benefits.** Beyond the applicants themselves.
* **Proportionate budget.** The cost matches the work.

## Key dates

* **October 30, 2026**: Applications open

* **December 31, 2026**: Application deadline

* **January–March 2027**: Review period

* **Mid-March 2027**: Applicants notified

* **April–September 2027**: Anticipated project period

Questions about eligibility or the application process are welcome. [Email the programme](mailto:contact@researchsoftwarecatalyst.fund). We cannot review ideas or say whether a proposal is in scope before you submit; see the [FAQ](/faq/#pre-submission-feedback).
