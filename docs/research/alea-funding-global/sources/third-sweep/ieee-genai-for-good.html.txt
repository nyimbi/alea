Start Narrative Application Start Technical Application The Use Cases We’re looking for teams ready to put their GenAI expertise to work — where cities need it most. This Challenge centers on three real-world, city-owned use cases across Europe and Central Asia, each shaped by a partner city’s own priorities within the green and digital transition. Modernizing Transit Route Reviews City: Pristina, Kosovo 1 Pristina is growing fast, and its bus routes need regular review to keep pace. This use case explores a multilingual GenAI assistant that helps municipal planners pull together transport routes, traffic data, and residents’ feedback so that the public transit system can adapt as the city grows. View Use Case Unlocking Energy Efficiency Investments City: Rivne, Ukraine Residents, homeowner associations and small businesses in Rivne want to cut costs and build energy resilience, but don’t know where to start. This use case explores a GenAI-powered energy efficiency assistant that walks users from a building profile to indicative costs, financing options, and a ready-to-share report. View Use Case Safeguarding Green Infrastructure City: Skopje, North Macedonia Skopje’s digital Green Cadastre keeps track of the city’s green infrastructure, including trees and green spaces. However, the records behind it are scattered across formats and departments. This use case explores how GenAI could connect those records to the Cadastre to support decision-making, ultimately safeguarding the city’s green infrastructure. You’ll create a free Zindi account, complete a hands-on task, and your results will help reviewers understand vour team’s ability to bring your solution to life Access Zindi >> Phases & Evaluation Criteria Phase 1 (Open Call): Technical Skills Assessment: Candidates are filtered by performance through a skills assessment on the Zindi Platform. The top 100 technical performers move to panel evaluation Narrative Submission Assessment A panel reviews narrative submissions using the following core criteria: Use Case Alignment (20%): The application demonstrates a clear understanding of the city use case and showing how the proposed solution directly addresses its key challenges. Impact Potential (25%): The application demonstrates expected value and outcome of deploying the solution using a responsible, inclusive, and open-source design. Team Capability (20%): The application demonstrates technical expertise, related experience, and readiness to collaborate with the use case owner. Technical Approach (35%): The application demonstrates the technical feasibility of the proposed solution, its innovative methodology, and the appropriate use of data and technology, including how the team plans to incorporate GENIE AI. Phase 2 (Semi-Finalists): Reviewers will evaluate the updated narrative proposals, reflecting key insights and expertise gained during the Use Case Sprint, using the core evaluation categories listed in Phase 1 (Technical Approach, Impact Potential, Use Case Alignment, and Team Capability). Phase 3 (Finalists): Reviewers will evaluate the prototypes and narrative applications and live pitch presentation on the core evaluation categories listed in Phase 1 (Technical Approach, Impact Potential, Use Case Alignment, and Team Capability).
