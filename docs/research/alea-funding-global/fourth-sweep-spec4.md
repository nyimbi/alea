# Fourth funding sweep: what the spec4 series adds

2026-11-25. Method: six standard-mode web searches. Every row below rests on search snippets only. I fetched and archived none of these pages, so none may be cited in an application until the primary page is read.

## What spec4 changes

The spec4 series (`docs/spec/spec4-base.md`, `spec4-ext.md`, `spec4-data-collection.md`, `base4-intf.md`) specifies a platform for 20 user groups: citizens (WhatsApp and USSD), journalists (story detection), researchers and educators, SMEs, financial institutions, cities, humanitarian and development bodies, and telecoms. It adds an open data commons, citizen science, and a freemium commercial layer. Earlier sweeps searched for energy-modelling funders. spec4 opens five more classes of funder: digital public goods, journalism and civic data, climate early warning and resilience, mobile and telecom, and AI for development.

## Does spec4 raise the number of grants to apply for?

No, not now. Three reasons.

1. **No new open calls.** The three applications in `docs/applications/` remain the only open and eligible calls found.
2. **Capacity.** One contributor, a single-country-first rule, and a defect register with 13 open items. Each grant promises deliverables. A fourth application before the first three are submitted would dilute them.
3. **spec4 mostly describes a product, not a research project.** Citizens, SMEs and cities are served by revenue (freemium, consulting, API tiers), which spec4-ext already plans. Grants fit the open-data and verification parts.

## What to do instead

- **Cheap and non-monetary:** list ALEA in the Digital Public Goods registry once a stable release exists. Check the eligibility standard first.
- **Pipeline for 2027:** AFD Digital Energy Challenge, GSMA Innovation Fund, Lacuna Fund, Google.org AI for Science, LEAP-SE 2027, each through the applicant that fits (Datacraft for the SME calls, the individual for the personal ones, a Kenyan university or consortium for the research ones).
- **Partnerships before applications:** Code for Africa (journalism layer), GridWatch (outage labels), a Kenyan university.
- **Commercial layer, not a grant:** the spec4 freemium and consulting plan is the revenue path for citizen, SME and city features.
